<Lede>

A relay stores events and hands them to whoever asks. Armada's communities ride
across relays gift-wrapped, so a relay carrying yours holds **sealed envelopes
addressed to nobody it can identify** — and it is not the only one holding them.

</Lede>

<Section mark="relay">

## You probably do not need one

Any Nostr relay carries an Armada community. [nostr.watch](https://nostr.watch)
lists hundreds, run by people who have never heard of yours, and a community
spread across a few of them survives any one of them going down.

Running your own does not make the community yours, faster, or more private. It
adds a machine you now have to keep online, and if you point your members at
*only* that machine you have rebuilt the single point of failure you left
Discord to escape.

The good reasons are the public ones: the network is made of relays, and there
are not many operators. Run one because other people's events should have
somewhere to go.

</Section>

<Section mark="terminal">

## ditto-relay

Soapbox runs [ditto-relay](https://gitlab.com/soapbox-pub/ditto-relay):
AGPL-3.0, written for Bun, storing events in OpenSearch. It is the heavy
option — full-text search (NIP-50), negentropy sync (NIP-77), expiration,
protected events, Prometheus metrics — and it is what `relay.ditto.pub` is.

<Callout>

It ships no Dockerfile and no compose file, so you are writing the service
definition and running an OpenSearch 2.x cluster yourself. Two settings catch
everyone: `NOSTR_NSEC` must be the `nsec…` form, and the port is `13131`.

</Callout>

If you want a relay rather than a search engine, the small implementations are
the better first box: [strfry](https://github.com/hoytech/strfry) (C++, LMDB,
runs on almost nothing) and
[nostr-rs-relay](https://github.com/scsibug/nostr-rs-relay) (Rust, SQLite) both
carry Armada traffic without a cluster underneath them.

</Section>

<Section mark="decks">

## Point Armada at it

Users add relays under **Settings → App relays**, and that list is theirs. To
ship a default with a client you host, build with `VITE_APP_RELAYS` — see
[the web app's configuration](/self-hosting/web-app/#configuration).

Note what the app relays are actually for: profiles, lists, and other ordinary
Nostr traffic. A community's own traffic follows the relay list that travels
with its invite — **Settings → Community relays** — so adding a relay to the app
list is not how somebody joins your community.

</Section>
